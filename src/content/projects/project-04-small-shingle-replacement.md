    tone: dark
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        The roof deck is the structural surface everything else attaches
        to. New shingles installed over a deck with hidden rot or soft
        spots won't perform the way they're supposed to, no matter how good
        the shingle itself is.
      - >-
        That's why a deck inspection during tear-off matters as much as the
        shingle installation that follows it.
