**Cleaning, maintenance, or inspection?** They are not the same visit. Roof cleaning removes moss, algae, and debris from the surface. Maintenance is broader: it includes cleaning, and also checks flashing, vents, gutters, and general condition so small issues get caught early. An inspection is the one that documents the roof's condition. Picking the right one up front means the visit answers the question you actually have.

**What a maintenance visit can turn up.** If JBA finds a problem during a maintenance visit, it points it out, shows you photos, and explains what it is and how urgent it is. Some issues are minor enough to note and monitor, and others are worth scheduling a repair for. JBA says it won't pressure you into work the roof doesn't need during a routine maintenance visit.

**What maintenance can and can't do.** Keeping moss, debris, and clogged gutters under control reduces the moisture and weight problems that shorten a roof's life, especially in Western Washington's wet climate. It won't extend a roof past its material lifespan, but it can help you avoid preventable damage that leads to an earlier repair or replacement than necessary.

**How often.** It depends on tree cover and how much moss or debris the roof typically collects. Roofs with heavy shade or overhanging trees may need cleaning more often than roofs with more sun, and JBA can give you a specific recommendation after taking a look. The [maintenance plans](/maintenance-plans/) page lays out the options.

**When maintenance isn't the answer.** Moss alone doesn't decide it. Widespread wear, repeated repairs in the same spots, or a roof well past its expected lifespan points toward [replacement](/services/roof-replacement/), and the first photo below shows a mossy roof that ended up there. An [inspection](/services/roof-inspection/) is how you find out which side of that line your roof is on.

Start with [roof cleaning](/services/roof-cleaning/) for moss and debris or [roof maintenance](/services/roof-maintenance/) for a fuller check, choose [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/) for damage, and [request an estimate](/assessment/) when you're ready.
