**Serving Anacortes.** JBA Roofing takes roofing jobs for homeowners in Anacortes. This page is about homes in the city of Anacortes. Reach out through the [contact page](/contact/) or [start an assessment](/assessment/) with your address.

**Step one: assess.** A replacement starts with a roof inspection, to confirm replacement is actually the right call. If the roof just needs cleaning or a small repair, that's what JBA recommends.

**Step two: plan.** This is where material selection, scope, and a written quote come together. A good quote explains the material and its quality, what is being torn off and down to what layer, what happens (and what it costs) if decking problems are found, a realistic timeline, and what is included, such as cleanup and disposal.

**Step three: protect.** Landscaping and driveways are covered before tear-off begins.

**Step four: install.** Tear-off, a look at the deck, and installation of the new roofing system. New roofing sits directly on the deck, so rotted or damaged decking has to be replaced first, and its condition often can't be confirmed until the old roof is off. JBA shows you what it found before proceeding with any decking work, instead of billing it as a surprise on the final invoice.

**Step five: document.** A final walkthrough, with photos of the completed roof.

**What can change the scope.** The overall condition of the roof, what the decking turns out to be, roof lines such as valleys and dormers, penetrations such as vents, chimneys, and skylights (each needs its own flashing detail), the material you choose, and access to the property all shape a replacement.

**Getting the house ready.** Move cars away from the driveway and work area, clear patio furniture and décor near the house, and move valuables stored in the attic or near exterior walls. Tear-off and installation days are loud, and JBA walks you through the specific timeline beforehand.

Start with a [roof inspection](/services/roof-inspection/), look at [roof replacement](/services/roof-replacement/), or choose [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/) if the damage is localized. The wider [Skagit County](/service-areas/skagit-county/) page covers the county as a whole.
