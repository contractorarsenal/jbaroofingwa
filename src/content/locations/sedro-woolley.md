**Serving Sedro-Woolley.** JBA Roofing takes roofing jobs for homeowners in Sedro-Woolley. This page is about homes in the city of Sedro-Woolley. Reach out through the [contact page](/contact/) or [start an assessment](/assessment/) with your address.

**Step one: stop the water.** If a roof is actively leaking, JBA's first priority is stopping water from getting further into the house. If water is coming in right now, treat it as urgent and call or start a repair request right away. A stain that isn't growing can usually wait for a scheduled inspection.

**Step two: look at the whole roof.** JBA gets a full look at the roof, not just the spot that's leaking, and documents what it finds. It explains the repair approach before starting, so you know what is being done and why.

**Step three: the lasting fix.** Once conditions allow a proper bond, meaning dry decking and workable weather, the lasting repair is completed. How long that takes depends on the specific issue, the weather, and scheduling, and JBA gives you a realistic estimate once it has seen the roof.

**When water has reached the decking.** Once water gets past the roofing material and into the decking, the wood can absorb moisture and weaken even if the surface damage looks minor from above. That decking needs to dry out and, in damaged sections, be replaced before a repair can properly seal the area again. The photos below follow one real case.

**Does a leak mean a new roof?** Not necessarily. Most leaks come from a specific, localized problem that a repair can resolve, especially when the rest of the roof is sound. Replacement becomes the better answer when damage is widespread, repairs keep failing, or the roof is significantly aged, and an inspection is what tells you which situation applies.

Start with [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/), ask for a [roof inspection](/services/roof-inspection/) if you want the whole roof checked, or look at [roof replacement](/services/roof-replacement/) if the roof is at the end of its life.
