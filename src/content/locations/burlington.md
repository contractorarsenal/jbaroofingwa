**Serving Burlington.** JBA Roofing takes roofing jobs for homeowners in Burlington. This page is about homes in the city of Burlington. Reach out through the [contact page](/contact/) or [start an assessment](/assessment/) with your address.

**Repair or replace?** One damaged area on a roof with plenty of life left is usually a repair, and missing or damaged shingles in a specific area can often be replaced without touching the rest of the roof. Widespread wear, repeated repairs in the same spots, or a roof already past its expected lifespan usually points toward [replacement](/services/roof-replacement/). A [roof inspection](/services/roof-inspection/) gives you a straight answer rather than a default toward the bigger job.

**What a replacement involves.** The old roofing comes off down to the deck, so the deck's condition can be seen before anything new goes on. Where decking is damaged or rotted, it has to be replaced first, and JBA shows you what it finds before proceeding. Only then does installation of the new roofing begin, following the manufacturer's specifications for the chosen shingle product. The photos below show those stages.

**Questions for the estimate.** Ask the estimate to name the material and quality, say what is being torn off, explain how rotted decking would be handled, give a realistic timeline, and spell out cleanup and disposal. If any of that is missing, ask before you sign.

**Getting the house ready.** On replacement days, move vehicles away from the driveway and work area, clear patio furniture and fragile items near the house, and move valuables stored in the attic or near exterior walls. Tear-off and installation are loud, so plan around that, and keep pets and kids away from the work zone until the crew finishes for the day.

For a leak or damaged shingles, start with [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/). The wider [Skagit County](/service-areas/skagit-county/) page covers the county as a whole.
