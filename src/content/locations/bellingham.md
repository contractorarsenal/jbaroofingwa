**Serving Bellingham.** JBA Roofing takes roofing jobs for homeowners in Bellingham. This page is about homes in the city of Bellingham. Reach out through the [contact page](/contact/) or [start an assessment](/assessment/) with your address.

**If you see moss, algae, or debris.** That points toward [roof cleaning](/services/roof-cleaning/) or [roof maintenance](/services/roof-maintenance/). Cleaning applies a low-pressure treatment to kill growth at the root, then clears the treated growth and debris from the roof surface and gutters. Maintenance is broader: a roof walk, debris removal, gutter clearing, and moss treatment where needed, annually or any time you notice moss, debris buildup, or clogged gutters. Neither is a substitute for repairing existing damage. If moss has already lifted shingle edges and water has reached the decking, that is a repair.

**If water is getting in, or shingles are damaged.** That points toward [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/): leaks, damaged or missing shingles, flashing problems, and storm damage. If a roof is actively leaking, stopping the water comes first, then a full look at the roof, then the lasting fix. A repair makes sense when the damage is localized and the rest of the roof is sound.

**If the roof is worn out across the board.** That points toward [roof replacement](/services/roof-replacement/). The signs are a roof at or past its expected material lifespan, widespread granule loss, curling or cracking shingles, repeated repairs in the same areas, or storm damage too extensive for a targeted repair. Replacement is also where decking comes in: damaged decking has to be replaced before new roofing goes down, and JBA shows you what it found before proceeding.

**If you honestly can't tell.** Start with a [roof inspection](/services/roof-inspection/). Common times are buying or selling a home, after a storm, or when it has simply been a while. It covers the shingles and roofing surface, flashing, penetrations, visible damage, moss and buildup, drainage, roof edges, visible decking concerns, and the attic where it's accessible, and you get a summary of condition and recommendations. If the roof just needs cleaning or a small repair, that's what JBA recommends.
