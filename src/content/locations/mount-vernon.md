**Start by confirming coverage.** JBA extends its King and Snohomish County service into parts of Skagit County, and coverage isn't uniform across the whole county. Reach out through the [contact page](/contact/) or [start an assessment](/assessment/) with your address, and JBA confirms whether it covers your area before anything else.

**Services, once coverage is confirmed.** Where JBA covers your area, it offers the same services as elsewhere: roof replacement, repair, inspection, cleaning, and routine maintenance. The first step is an inspection to see what the roof actually needs, not a guess based on county lines.

**Moss and debris.** Moss needs shade and consistent moisture to establish itself, which is why it shows up most on north-facing slopes, roofs under heavy tree cover, and areas that stay damp between rain events. Roofs with heavy shade or overhanging trees may need [cleaning](/services/roof-cleaning/) more often than roofs with more sun. JBA can give a specific recommendation after taking a look, and [roof maintenance](/services/roof-maintenance/) covers the routine upkeep.

**When moss means more than cleaning.** Moss is often the most visible sign of an aging roof, but the decision to replace comes down to the overall condition of the roofing system, not moss alone. The first project photo below shows a mossy, aging roof that was replaced; an inspection is what tells you which side of that line your roof is on.

**Permits.** It depends on the property and the scope of the project. JBA checks what applies to your specific job once coverage for your address is confirmed, and handles the permit process if one is needed.

Start with a [roof inspection](/services/roof-inspection/), choose [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/) for a leak or damaged shingles, or ask about [roof replacement](/services/roof-replacement/) if the roof is at the end of its life.
