  - eyebrow: The Checklist
    heading: What Do We Look At?
    intro: "A roof inspection covers what's visible and reasonably accessible, including:"
    items:
      - title: Shingles and roofing surface
        description: Overall wear, granule loss, cracking, curling, or missing material.
      - title: Flashing
        description: Condition of flashing around chimneys, vents, and valleys.
      - title: Penetrations
        description: Vent pipes, skylights, and other roof-mounted fixtures.
      - title: Visible damage
        description: Any signs of impact, wind lift, or storm-related wear.
      - title: Moss and buildup
        description: Growth or debris that could be holding moisture against the roof.
      - title: Drainage
        description: Whether water has a clear path off the roof.
      - title: Roof edges
        description: Condition of edges and eaves, where wear often shows first.
      - title: Visible decking concerns
        description: Signs of decking issues where accessible, without opening up the roof.
